# CRYPTOLENS — Project Review Checklist

Use this worksheet to organize research. A completed checklist is **not** a safety score and does not mean a project is a good investment.

## Identity and documentation
- [ ] Official website and social channels cross-checked
- [ ] Team identity and relevant track record checked where verifiable
- [ ] Whitepaper, technical docs and roadmap read
- [ ] Product exists and claims can be independently tested

## Token design and supply
- [ ] Token utility is clearly described
- [ ] Circulating, total and maximum supply distinguished
- [ ] Contract address confirmed from an official source
- [ ] Team, investor and ecosystem allocations identified
- [ ] Vesting and unlock dates checked against primary sources

## Security and governance
- [ ] Audit report read, including scope and date
- [ ] Unresolved audit findings checked
- [ ] Admin keys, upgrade controls and multisig arrangements understood
- [ ] Governance rights and voting concentration reviewed
- [ ] Incident history and bug bounty checked

## Market structure and risks
- [ ] Liquidity and trading venues independently checked
- [ ] Holder concentration examined
- [ ] Conflicts of interest and paid promotion identified
- [ ] Legal, jurisdictional and tax questions listed
- [ ] Key claims recorded with source links and date checked

## Decision hygiene
- [ ] Missing information listed
- [ ] Bear case written down
- [ ] No decision made solely from social-media hype
- [ ] Risk of total loss understood

**Reminder:** This is an educational research aid, not investment, legal or tax advice.
